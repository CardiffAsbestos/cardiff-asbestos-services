What we remove

We specialise in lower-risk, non-licensed asbestos work. If you suspect a material contains asbestos, leave it undisturbed and get in touch. We will tell you what it is likely to be and the safest next step.

Higher-risk materials such as sprayed coatings, lagging and insulating board must be removed by an HSE-licensed contractor. If your job falls into that category, we will say so and point you in the right direction.

  • Asbestos cement roofing and sheets garages, sheds, outbuildings
  • Soffits, fascias and cladding domestic and commercial
  • Textured coatings ceilings and walls
  • Vinyl and thermoplastic floor tiles with bitumen adhesive
  • Flue pipes and rainwater goods cement-based
  • Cold water tanks and panels cement-based

Common questions

How do I know if a material contains asbestos?

You cannot tell by looking. A sample analysed by a specialist laboratory is the only reliable way to confirm it. We can arrange sampling, or assume asbestos and treat the material accordingly.

Does asbestos always have to be removed?

No. Asbestos in good condition that will not be disturbed can often be left and managed. We will advise on whether removal is needed given its condition and your plans.

What is non-licensed asbestos work?

Lower-risk work, such as removing asbestos cement or textured coatings, that does not require an HSE licence but still has to be done with the right controls, equipment and waste handling.

Can you work on my home?

Yes. Most of our domestic jobs are garage and shed roofs, soffits and fascias, and textured ceilings.

Do you work with councils and housing providers?

Yes. We provide documented removal for local authority and housing properties across South Wales.

What happens to the waste?

It is double-wrapped, labelled and taken to a permitted disposal site. We give you the paperwork for your records.
