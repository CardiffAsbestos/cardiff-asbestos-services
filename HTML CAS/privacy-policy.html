What we do

We remove non-licensed asbestos containing materials for homeowners, businesses and local authorities across South Wales. To do this we need to collect some personal information about the people who contact us and the properties we work on.

Information we collect

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, email address and, for businesses and councils, your job title and organisation.
  • Property and job details: the address or postcode of the property, a description of the work, and any photos, plans or surveys you send us.
  • Quotes and job records: our communications with you, quotes, dates of work, access arrangements and sign-off.
  • Waste and compliance records: waste transfer or consignment paperwork, which includes the address where the material was removed.
  • Payment records: invoices and confirmation of payment. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Website data: when you visit our site, technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested is processed by our website host so that the site loads and stays secure.

How we collect it

  • Directly from you, when you fill in our enquiry form, call, email or meet us.
  • From businesses, housing providers and local authorities that instruct us, about their properties and the contacts involved.
  • From laboratories or surveyors, if sampling or a survey has been carried out on your property.

Our enquiry form works by opening your own email app with your message ready to send. Nothing is stored on our website itself. Your message reaches us only when you press send in your email app.

How and why we use your information

We only use personal information where the law allows it. Our reasons are:

  • To take steps at your request before a contract, and to carry out a contract: responding to enquiries, arranging visits, preparing quotes, carrying out the work and invoicing.
  • To meet our legal obligations: keeping waste and accounting records, health and safety duties, and responding to lawful requests from regulators.
  • For our legitimate interests: running and improving our business, keeping records of the work we have done, protecting our website and handling complaints or disputes. We balance these interests against your rights.
  • With your consent: only where the law requires it, for example if we ever want to send you marketing. You can withdraw consent at any time.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use automated decision-making or profiling.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only where needed, with:

  • Waste carriers and permitted disposal sites that handle the waste we remove.
  • Laboratories and surveyors, if we arrange sampling or testing.
  • Subcontractors working with us on your job.
  • Our accountants, insurers and professional advisers.
  • Service providers that support our business, such as our email provider (Microsoft), our website host and DNS provider (Cloudflare) and Google, which supplies the fonts used on our site.
  • Regulators, councils or the police, where we are legally required to or the law allows us to.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on the safeguards the law provides, such as approved transfer mechanisms.

Cookies

We do not currently use advertising or analytics cookies on our website. If we add them in future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required. Our website loads fonts from Google, which means your IP address is sent to Google when the page loads.

How long we keep your information

  • Enquiries that do not go ahead: up to 12 months.
  • Job, invoice and payment records: normally 6 years, to meet tax and accounting requirements and to deal with any later claims.
  • Waste documentation: for as long as the law requires.

When we no longer need information, we delete it or make it anonymous.

Keeping your information safe

We use sensible measures to protect your information, including password-protected accounts and devices, limited access and secure disposal of paper records. No system is completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive information by email unless necessary.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Ask us to delete your information, where we have no good reason to keep it.
  • Ask us to restrict how we use your information.
  • Object to our use of your information based on legitimate interests.
  • Ask for your information in a portable format, where it applies.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on it.

To use any of these rights, email cardiff-asbestos@outlook.com. We will respond within one month. We may need to confirm your identity first.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Children

Our services are not aimed at children, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy
